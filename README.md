# 🎬 Cinema Paradiso

### Every film deserves an audience.

**Movies are better together.**

Cinema Paradiso is a real-time watch-party platform that brings the experience of watching movies with friends into a shared digital space. Create a room, invite your friends, discover movies, and experience every moment together, wherever you are.

## 🎞️ Why Cinema Paradiso?

Watching a movie is more than just pressing play. It's about reacting to unexpected twists, laughing at the same scene, discussing a character, and sharing the moments that make a film memorable.

But watching remotely often loses that feeling of being together.

Cinema Paradiso was built around a simple idea: **distance shouldn't take away the shared experience of cinema.**

By bringing synchronized playback, real-time conversations, and movie discovery into one cinematic space, Cinema Paradiso makes watching together feel more natural.

## ✨ Features

- **🎟️ Private Watch Rooms:** Create a room and invite friends using a unique room code.
- **▶️ Synchronized Playback:** Keep YouTube playback coordinated across participants.
- **💬 Live Chat:** Share thoughts and discuss moments in real time while watching.
- **🪑 Interactive Theatre:** See who's watching with you through participant names and a seating-inspired interface.
- **🎬 Movie Discovery:** Explore movie posters and information powered by TMDB.
- **⏱️ Playback Sync Indicator:** See whether participants are slightly ahead of or behind the host.
- **😀 Emoji Picker:** Express reactions and add personality to conversations.
- **🌙 Cinematic Design:** A theatre-inspired interface built around atmosphere, deep colours, and the feeling of a shared screening.

## ⚙️ How It Works

**1. Create a room**

Start a watch party and receive a room code to share with friends.

**2. Bring everyone together**

Friends join the same room, where participants are visible in the theatre interface.

**3. Choose what to watch**

Discover movies through TMDB and select content available through YouTube.

**4. Experience it together**

Watch with synchronized playback, chat in real time, and react to the moments that matter.

## 🎨 The Vision

Remember the old-school cinema?

The dimming lights. The velvet seats. The glow of the screen cutting through the darkness. The quiet anticipation before the opening scene, the laughter that travels across the rows, the collective gasp when a story takes an unexpected turn. And when the credits roll, the conversations that follow long after the lights come back on.

**That feeling is what Cinema Paradiso wants to bring to your screen.**

We imagined a theatre without walls, where your friends could take their seats from anywhere in the world. A place where choosing a film feels like picking tonight's screening, where every person has a place in the audience, and where the experience is about more than simply watching the same video.

Cinema Paradiso brings the spirit of the classic cinema into a digital space, blending the nostalgia of the old theatre with the ease of watching online. The curtains may be invisible, and the seats may be thousands of miles apart, but the audience is still together.

Because technology can bring a screen closer. **We want it to bring people closer, too.**

**One room. One screen. An audience, together.**
